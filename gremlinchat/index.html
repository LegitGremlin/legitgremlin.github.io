GremGone

First of all, thank you to anyone who used the platform because development would've ceased immediately without any users. The dedication of my development to the platform is easily outranked by the dedication of the users to have used it on the daily.

Unfortunately, due to the request by the administration of the environment GremChat primarily thrived in, the app is permanently discontinued.

The app itself in source is in no state to compete with Discord on a grand level but it has another use. You may or may not see GremChat's unique UI design on another website within the next few years as it grows beyond a localized app, as the source code may be repurposed into a proper self-hosted alternative to Discord similar to Revolt/Stoat.



As for anyone who used my original version of GremChat anywhere from Sep7/2026 to Oct21/2026:

We had a good run, but we have to move on now, without GremChat.
